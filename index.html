Our Story

Every piece begins with a pair of hands.

SAJORA Boutique was born from a simple belief: that jewelry should feel personal, not mass-produced. We partner with a small circle of Bangladeshi artisans who hand-string, hand-set, and hand-polish every bangle, churi, and heirloom in our collection.

From traditional glass churi in jewel tones to bridal sets in gold and pearl, each SAJORA piece carries the fingerprints of its maker — and the story of the woman who wears it.
